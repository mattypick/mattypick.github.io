Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Journey Monitor is an iPhone journey-monitoring app developed by Matthew Pickford. Contact: mattypick@outlook.com. No Journey Monitor account is required.

What the app uses

The app uses your precise location, selected destination and route progress to calculate and monitor journeys that you choose to start. An explicitly active journey can use location while the app is in the background or the phone is locked. Ending monitoring stops active journey location monitoring.

Information sent to service providers

Routing, destination search and traffic requests send the information needed for that request to TomTom over HTTPS. This can include your current or chosen start location, destination and route. Optional predictive traffic checks may send a locally learned usual start area and destination near an expected departure time.

The selected map provider receives requests for the map area visible on screen and your network address. Apple Maps uses Apple services; TomTom uses its map-tile services; optional HERE maps use HERE raster-tile and supplier-notice services when configured. Changing the base map does not change the TomTom routing and traffic engine.

Journey Monitor does not operate its own app-data server or receive a separate copy of these provider requests. Provider terms cover location-bearing requests, search queries, timestamps, network addresses and transaction logs used to provide, secure and troubleshoot services. TomTom’s terms also permit aggregated or de-identified data to be used for analytics and service improvement. The absence of our own server does not mean that no information is collected by service providers. Providers process requests under their published terms and privacy practices: TomTom, Apple and HERE.

Information kept on your iPhone

Favourites, recent destinations, preferences, active-journey restoration data and Smart Suggestion learning are stored locally. Learning uses journey starts, destinations and timing rather than a permanent detailed driving track. The finished-journey summary is temporary. Map responses may be cached locally to reduce repeated downloads.

Purchases

Apple processes Journey Monitor Plus purchases. The app uses StoreKit to check access and restore purchases. It does not receive card or other payment details and does not upload subscription records to its own server.

Advertising, tracking and analytics

Journey Monitor contains no advertising or third-party advertising/analytics SDKs and does not use information for cross-app tracking or sell personal data. These statements do not exclude service-provider request logging or the aggregated or de-identified analytics described above.

Retention and deletion

Active-journey restoration data is removed when monitoring ends. You can remove favourites and recent destinations in the app and reset learned suggestions in Settings. Deleting the app removes its locally stored app data. Any device or iCloud backups are managed separately through Apple.

Deleting the app does not delete service-provider logs. HERE states that its Location Services APIs are ordinarily stateless, but security and troubleshooting logs may be kept for up to 30 days. TomTom’s retention is governed by its service terms and data-processing schedule. See HERE’s service data-processing details and TomTom’s data-processing schedule. These schedules describe provider processing; Journey Monitor does not send an advertising identifier, an app-account identifier or a separate driving-history upload.

Your choices

You can change notification categories, sounds, map layers, Smart Suggestions and predictive alerts in the app. Predictive alerts are optional and off by default. You can change Location, Precise Location, Notifications and Live Activity permissions in iOS Settings. Disabling Location prevents location-dependent monitoring until permission is restored.

Support contact

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send will be used to respond to your request. Please do not send API keys, passwords or unnecessary precise location information. Contact mattypick@outlook.com for privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence.
