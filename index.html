A companion for the journey

Journey Monitor uses TomTom routes and traffic to follow progress, notice meaningful traffic changes and alert you as you approach delays. It is a journey monitor, not turn-by-turn navigation.

Choose what you see and hear

Choose your map display and traffic overlay. Adjust alert categories and sounds before you travel. Optional Journey Monitor Plus adds Live Activities, Dynamic Island, a live Apple Watch journey view and additional controls.

Background checks depend on iOS runtime, location access and connectivity. Updates and alerts are not guaranteed at exact intervals. Set up your journey before driving and do not interact with your phone while driving.

Private by design

No Journey Monitor account is required. Saved places and suggestion learning stay on your iPhone. Location information needed for routing, traffic and maps is sent to the relevant service providers; the privacy policy explains these requests.
