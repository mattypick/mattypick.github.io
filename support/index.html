Common questions

Traffic or ETA stopped updating

Check that the journey is still active, Location and Precise Location are enabled, and the phone has internet access. Journey Monitor aims to refresh traffic about every two minutes when iOS grants supported background runtime; it is not an exact background timer. Force-quitting the app may stop active monitoring. Reopen the app and check the journey.

When requests fail, the app keeps the last valid route and traffic. Older traffic is marked as potentially out of date. Once connected, use Refresh Traffic.

I’m not hearing or feeling alerts

Check iOS Settings → Journey Monitor → Notifications, Focus and silent settings. In Journey Monitor, open Settings → Traffic Alerts → Sound & Haptics. Use Send Test Notification before travelling. iOS controls the vibration pattern outside the app; in-app haptic choices apply only while the app is visible. Apple Watch may receive an alert instead of a locked iPhone.

Time Sensitive driving alerts are optional and subject to iOS permissions. They are not Critical Alerts and do not override the silent switch.

Live Activity or Watch journey is missing

The live journey view on Apple Watch and Live Activity are Plus features. Check Plus access and Settings → Journey → Live Activity. Check iOS Live Activity permissions, reopen the iPhone app and confirm a journey is active. Normal notification mirroring is controlled by Apple.

The map and route delay look different

Map Layers changes the base map, not the TomTom route engine. Apple’s wider-area traffic and TomTom’s route-specific information can differ. Traffic delay may exist without a separately reported incident. Switching map layers does not restart monitoring.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Open Settings → Journey Monitor Plus → Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that purchased it. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Access follows Apple’s entitlement state; cancellation does not delete saved places. Availability, prices and any introductory offer are shown by the App Store.

Why are Smart Suggestions empty?

They learn locally from journeys you actually start and need repeated patterns. Enable suggestions in Settings if you have Plus. Predictive traffic notifications are separate, optional and off by default; iOS decides when their background checks run.

How do I delete my information?

Remove favourites and recents in the app. Reset Learned Suggestions in Settings to remove learning without deleting favourites. End monitoring to remove the saved active journey. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data; device backups are managed separately by Apple. No Journey Monitor account needs deleting.

Journey Monitor is not an emergency service or a guarantee of road conditions. Set up before driving. Do not interact with the app while driving.
